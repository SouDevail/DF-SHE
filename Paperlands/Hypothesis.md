# Hypotheses 🧪
***(Last updated: September 2026)***

*For potential underlying theories, continue to [Theories.md](https://github.com/SouDevail/DF-SHE/blob/main/Paperlands/Theories.md).*

---

DF-SHE is built to challenge its own assumptions.

### 1. Community architecture

Hypothetically, could architecture become a new standard through community development rather than scale alone?

### 2. Resource degradation

Hypothetically, could resource degradation become a source of architectural progress?

### 3. Multilingual architecture

Hypothetically, could multiple programming languages inside the architecture create advantages beyond low-level work?

### 4. Optimisation

Hypothetically, could an optimisation make an AI more efficient while making it less capable?

### 5. Independent self-healing

Hypothetically, could self-healing become an independent part of training, officially associated with and recommended for DF-SHE, while using mechanisms that go beyond existing approaches and exploring how they could improve the resilience and behaviour of the resulting system?

---

*None of these are answers.....*

They are hypotheses waiting for evidence.
