# Theories 📼
***(Last updated: September 2026)***

*For abstract hypotheses, continue to [Hypothesis.md](https://github.com/SouDevail/DF-SHE/blob/main/Paperlands/Hypothesis.md).*

---

DF-SHE is built around several theories that I want to test rather than assume to be true.

### 1. Theoretical balance between small and large AI

Theoretically, AI does not have to be divided simply into “small” and “large”. A sufficiently organised architecture might find a different balance between capability and resource use.

### 2. Theoretical scaling through organisation

Theoretically, scaling could come from organisation rather than only from computation. How far can resources be reduced before reasoning and coherence begin to suffer?

### 3. Theoretical independence of specialists

Theoretically, Nano, Micro and Macro specialists could be trained independently and inserted into the architecture without rebuilding the whole system.

### 4. Theoretical security through hierarchy

Theoretically, a deeper hierarchy could behave differently from conventional expert routing under adversarial pressure. My experience with AI red teaming made me question whether routing can always make the right decision.

### 5. Theoretical limits of further decomposition

Theoretically, dividing the architecture even further could bring maximum resource efficiency — but also introduce new risks. Smaller experts might become easier for a router to misdirect, potentially creating new vulnerabilities to jailbreaks or unsafe responses.

One possible solution is to keep the smallest experts away from important knowledge and sensitive responsibilities, using them mainly for simple, low-level tasks.

The broader theory is simple:

**Different tasks may deserve different architectural advantages.**

DF-SHE is an attempt to put those ideas together and find out where they lead!🔬
