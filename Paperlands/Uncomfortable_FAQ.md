# Uncomfortable-FAQ 🏴‍☠️
***(Last updated: September 2026)***

---

*Some questions are supposed to be uncomfortable. That's it.* ***Make it shine!***

---

**About the Author**

---

#️⃣1️⃣

**“Do you actually understand what you're doing, or are you just assembling other people's ideas?"**

— Maybe ideas can be taken and given a new name. But nobody can steal the voice, reasoning, experience, study, analysis and work that happened in my head and through my hands before it reached the project.

---

#️⃣2️⃣

**“Why should anyone take a one-person project seriously?”**

— You don't have to. The project should earn that through its work, evidence and openness.

---

#️⃣3️⃣

**“Isn't this too ambitious for one author?”**

— Maybe. I still prefer trying to understand how far it can go rather than deciding the limit before starting.

---

#️⃣4️⃣

**“What if you lose motivation?”**

— Then the project should not depend entirely on one person's motivation. That is one reason DF-SHE is intended to become community-based.

---

**About the Idea**

---

#️⃣5️⃣

**“Why aren't existing solutions enough?”**

— There is no universal answer. Some people will consider them enough, others won't, and some will remain somewhere between the two. Technology rarely stops at “enough”.

---

#️⃣6️⃣

**“What if your main assumption is wrong?”**

— Prove it.

The repository is public, and the community will be able to challenge it. Being proven wrong is not humiliation. It can become another step in the research.

---

#️⃣7️⃣

**“Are you trying to solve a problem that doesn't exist?”**

— Possibly. History has repeatedly shown that what seems impossible can become possible, and what seems obvious can be reconsidered. Sometimes the real question is whether we have understood the problem correctly.

---

#️⃣8️⃣

**“What if a simpler solution is better?”**

— Then the simpler solution deserves to be taken seriously. DF-SHE does not need complexity for its own sake.

---

**About Development**

---

#️⃣9️⃣

**“Isn't this just vibe coding?”**

— I use AI very often for code generation. That part is true.

But I do not use AI as an autonomous developer. I use it as a generator while the reasoning, architecture, analysis, decisions and responsibility remain with me.

I call this AI-architecting.

My AI tools can make technical mistakes because of things such as context overload or poor output quality. That is exactly why I continuously develop my own prompt engineering and challenge generated results rather than treating them as authority.

---

#️⃣🔟

**“If AI writes much of the code, what is actually yours?”**

— The code is only one part of the project.

The questions, reasoning, experiments, criticism, decisions and system-level thinking behind it are mine. I do not want an AI to think for me. I want it to extend what I can build.

---

**About Open Source**

---

#️⃣1️⃣1️⃣

**“Aren't you afraid someone will simply take the idea?”**

— No. I respect that.

Just respect the legal side, the license and open-source culture. :)

---

#️⃣1️⃣2️⃣

**“Why show weaknesses publicly?”**

— Because it is an unspoken alternative philosophy of the project: creating a visible history can push a field forward faster than hiding everything and only showing the victories.

Informally: maybe history can move AI faster than the speed of light. Haha.

---

#️⃣1️⃣3️⃣

**“What if nobody wants to use it?”**

— If that “nobody” is a part of the community, it means that part of the community sees the project differently. That does not automatically make the project weak.

Strong criticism can make the project stronger.

---

#️⃣1️⃣4️⃣

**“What if nobody joins the community?”**

— Then DF-SHE remains my independent creation.

I will continue developing it as deeply and quickly as my knowledge and hands allow. I do not deny that development could become slower without a community. That is exactly why I want its independence to become decentralised.

---

**The Questions That Hurt**

---

#️⃣1️⃣5️⃣

**“What if you're simply wrong?”**

— It happens. Even the largest companies and the most experienced people can be wrong.

Every mistake can become part of science and part of history.

---

#️⃣1️⃣6️⃣

**“What if the expected benchmark results never appear?”**

— Then the benchmarks tell us something real about the existing work.

If the expected results cannot be reproduced, DF-SHE may become a documented research failure rather than a serious product. I do not see that as losing. The result is still knowledge.

---

#️⃣1️⃣7️⃣

**“What if the whole idea fails?”**

— Then it failed publicly, honestly and verifiably.

That is still more useful than an idea that was never tested.

---

*I would rather leave evidence behind than protect an idea from being challenged.*
