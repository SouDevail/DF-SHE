# Motivation 🙌

DF-SHE did not begin with a plan to create a new AI architecture.

It began with a problem I knew personally.

This motivation is closely connected to the [Philosophy](https://github.com/SouDevail/DF-SHE/blob/main/Paperlands/Philosophy.md) behind DF-SHE — one asks where the idea could lead👁️‍🗨️, 

while the other explains why I started following it.✊

I kept seeing how modern AI increasingly assumes modern hardware. Powerful GPUs. NPUs. New devices. And for people with older or weaker machines, local AI can become extremely limited.

Then came the questions.

*—What if someone has an old device?  
-What if they need local AI?  
⁻What if privacy matters to them?  
—What if they want something genuinely useful, not just a narrow offline toy?  
-What if the problem is not the person's hardware, but the way we design AI architectures?*

I could improve something that already exists. Instead, I wanted to explore something new.

Because if we only improve existing ideas, how do we ever change what “new” means?

DF-SHE grew from that thought.

One of my motivations is to develop and test through resource-providing platforms, deliberately choosing the weakest reasonable virtual environments I can find. I want to push beyond simply being CPU-friendly — to explore whether resource degradation itself can become a form of progress.

My own hardware is modest too: FX-6300, 8 GB RAM, GT 1030, OS-limited.

That is why we started.
