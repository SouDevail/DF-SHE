# Philosophy 👁️‍🗨️

I do not want to build AI by simply giving it more.

🕐More parameters.?
🕐More hardware..?
🕑More money...?
🕝More computation....?
🕰️ = ⛓️💸⛓️

---

But what if intelligence could come from something else?

The human brain is where this thought begins. Billions of neurons, connected through synapses and organised across many scales — from microscopic processes to larger systems working together.

Why is intelligence organised this way?

What if AI did not have to be one large machine, or simply a collection of interchangeable experts?

What if it could have different kinds of organisation, different scales and different degrees of independence — closer to a structure than a crowd?

DF-SHE explores that question.

And one rule comes first:

**CPU-only.**

Can architecture do more when brute computational scale is no longer the answer?

I do not know yet.

**What if the human brain could become not only an inspiration for AI, but part of the thinking behind its architecture?**

•↓•

That is why DF-SHE exists.
