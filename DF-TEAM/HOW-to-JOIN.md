# HOW-to-JOIN

You are now in the DF-SHE contribution process.  
Here is how an idea, discovery or skill becomes a real contribution.

---

### I — • FIND •

**[ Choose your contribution role → ](./R-TalentChoose/)**

You don't have to be a developer. Find the role that best matches what you can actually bring to DF-SHE. 🫴

🫲 You have so many opportunities to discover your talent and find your path to becoming a public figure! 🫱

---

### II — <- CONTRIBUTE ->

Choose the route that fits what you want to bring.

**Issue** — for a problem, question, discovery or proposal.

Write what you found, why it matters and enough information for someone else to understand it.

*Quick shape:*  
`What happened? → Why does it matter? → What should we look at?`

[See how to create an Issue →](../Assets/classic-github-docs/issue-creating.md)

**Pull Request** — when you have already made a useful change.

Make the change, check it yourself, explain what you changed and why, then submit it for review.

*Quick shape:*  
`What changed? → Why? → What did you check?`

[See how to create a Pull Request →](../Assets/classic-github-docs/pull-request.md)

**Not sure which one?**

**[ Return to R-TalentChoose → ](./R-TalentChoose/)**  
Your role guide will point you toward the appropriate contribution route.

---

### III — { TRACE }

After your contribution, choose what happens to your connection with its history.

**--> DF-reMEMBER √**

Your accepted contribution becomes part of the *public DF-SHE contributor* history.

`DF-reMEMBER √` also means voluntarily joining the professional circle of other- *DF-reMEMBER* contributors. Different roles, countries and experience levels can become real professional connections — potentially leading to a real partnership.

**DF-Solo**

Your contribution is still accepted and respected.  
You simply choose to remain independent, without providing your contacts for further connection through the DF-SHE contributor circle.

Neither status makes a contribution more or less valuable.

---

### IV — | PROFILE |

For `DF-reMEMBER`, the contributor record contains:

**Required:** Name, Contribution, Status.  
**Optional:** Country, Citation, Public Contact.

Your public name can be your chosen public identity. If none is provided, your GitHub username may be used. If the contribution originated through another public identity, that identity may be used when appropriate.

A Citation is optional and may contain up to **100 characters excluding spaces**.

A Public Contact is optional, but strongly recommended for `DF-reMEMBER` if you want other contributors to be able to reach you.

*Example:*

```
Status: DF-reMEMBER
Name: Idol X
Contribution: Found and documented a reproducible issue in V2.2 routing.
Country: Germany
Citation: "Strange questions sometimes lead to useful discoveries."
Public Contact: github.com/IdolX ; example@mail.com
```

*Example only — replace it with your own information.*
