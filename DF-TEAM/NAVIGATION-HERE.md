### Short FAQ

**Need Experience?**  
— No. Inexperience opens new knowledge; experience opens potential information.

**What can I do?**  
— Ask? Test? Research? Document? Create? Challenge? Code?  
You have your hands — and ours. Bring the community interested in [what DF-SHE is trying to discover](../Paperlands/Philosophy.md).

**DF-reMEMBER or DF-Solo?**  
— `DF-reMEMBER` — public contributor history + voluntary professional circle.  
`DF-Solo` — contribution accepted, participation remains independent.

**Need Contact?**  
— No. Optional, but strongly recommended for `DF-reMEMBER` — it gives other contributors a direct way to find you, discuss your contribution, exchange ideas and potentially **start a real partnership**.

*Non-existent roles reveal the boundlessness of the human mind. Go forward.*
