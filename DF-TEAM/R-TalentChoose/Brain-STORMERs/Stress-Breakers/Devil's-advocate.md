# 📛 Devil's advocate

### What is a Devil's-advocate?

A Devil's-advocate is a person and a way to contribute.

Someone who takes the position of the hardest possible opponent of DF-SHE.

Not because they want the project to fail.

Because every serious idea should survive someone trying to break its reasoning.

---

A Devil's-advocate searches for contradictions, hidden assumptions, weak explanations and problems that are difficult or uncomfortable to discuss.

Their purpose is not to destroy.

Their purpose is to make the project stronger.

---

### Challenge what cannot defend itself

A Devil's-advocate does not look for random mistakes.

They search for places where reality can challenge the project's claims.

They ask:

*"Where is the proof?"*

*"What happens if this assumption is wrong?"*

*"Why should this decision be considered correct?"*

A strong criticism does not say:

*"This is bad. Remove it."*

A strong criticism says:

*"The project claims X, but reality shows Y. Explain why this difference exists."*

---

### Create pressure, not conflict

The purpose of criticism is pressure.

A Devil's-advocate intentionally creates difficult discussions where decisions, principles and explanations must prove themselves.

If an idea cannot survive examination, it needs improvement.

If it survives, it becomes stronger.

---

### Attack the argument, not the person

A Devil's-advocate can be strict and direct.

But the target is never a person.

The target is:

- unsupported claims;
- contradictions;
- unexplained decisions;
- hidden risks.

An opinion without reasoning is not criticism.

A precise challenge with evidence is.

---

### Search where people avoid looking

Some problems remain because nobody wants to question them.

Some assumptions survive because they became comfortable.

A Devil's-advocate looks there.

They ask the question others avoid and reveal the weakness nobody wanted to discuss.

Not to damage DF-SHE.

To prevent DF-SHE from hiding its own problems.

---

### How to contribute

Find a statement, decision or principle that cannot fully defend itself.

Explain:

- what was claimed;
- what creates doubt;
- why it matters;
- what requires an answer.

The goal is not to win a debate.

The goal is to force better answers.

### Devil's-advocate and [Questioner](../../DF-TEAM/R-TalentChoose/Brain-STORMERs/Questioner.md)

[Questioner](../../DF-TEAM/R-TalentChoose/Brain-STORMERs/Questioner.md) asks:

**"Why is it like this?"**

Devil's-advocate asks:

**"Can this survive being challenged?"**

Questioner explores unknown possibilities.

Devil's-advocate challenges existing explanations.

They create different types of pressure for DF-SHE.

---

### Devil's-advocate and [Angel's-advocate](../../DF-TEAM/R-TalentChoose/Brain-STORMERs/Stress-Breakers/Angel's-advocate.md)

Devil's-advocate attempts to break the argument.

[Angel's-advocate](../../DF-TEAM/R-TalentChoose/Brain-STORMERs/Stress-Breakers/Angel's-advocate.md) attempts to understand and defend the argument.

One searches for weaknesses.

The other searches for reasoning, context and strengths.

Together they create independent discussion where ideas are tested from opposite directions.

**Devil's-advocate is a contribution method, not a title or profession.**
