# 🔰 Angel's-advocate

### What is an Angel's-advocate?

An Angel's-advocate is a person and a way to contribute.

Someone who examines the side of an idea that may be lost during criticism.

They search for reasoning, context and constraints behind decisions.

Their purpose is to help ideas be understood completely before they are judged.

---

### Understand before rejecting

Every decision has a reason behind it.

An Angel's-advocate asks:

*"What problem was this decision trying to solve?"*

*"What limitations influenced this choice?"*

*"What context may be missing from this criticism?"*

A decision can have weaknesses and still have a reason to exist.

Understanding the reason does not remove criticism.

It makes the discussion more accurate.

---

### Find what criticism may overlook

An Angel's-advocate searches for:

- design intentions;
- accepted trade-offs;
- technical limitations;
- goals behind decisions.

The purpose is not to hide problems.

The purpose is to prevent incomplete conclusions.

---

### Create balance, not agreement

An Angel's-advocate does not defend every decision.

A good defense can acknowledge problems.

The goal is to examine ideas from multiple directions.

[Devil's-advocate](/DF-TEAM/R-TalentChoose/Brain-STORMERs/Stress-Breakers/Devil's-advocate.md) asks:

**"Can this survive being challenged?"**

Angel's-advocate asks:

**"Has this been fully understood before being challenged?"**

Both reveal different parts of DF-SHE.

---

### Defend the argument, not the person

An Angel's-advocate supports ideas through:

- reasoning;
- evidence;
- context;
- explanations.

The target is never a person.

A defense without reasoning is only an opinion.

---

### How to contribute

Find an idea, decision or principle that is being questioned.

Explain:

- why it exists;
- what problem it addresses;
- what context affects it;
- why it may still be valuable.

The goal is not to win a debate.

The goal is to make the discussion more complete.

Angel's-advocate reveals context behind existing decisions.

### Angel's-advocate and [Devil's-advocate](/DF-TEAM/R-TalentChoose/Brain-STORMERs/Stress-Breakers/Devil's-advocate.md)

[Devil's-advocate](/DF-TEAM/R-TalentChoose/Brain-STORMERs/Stress-Breakers/Devil's-advocate.md) searches for contradictions and weaknesses.

Angel's-advocate searches for reasoning and overlooked context.

One challenges existing explanations.

The other examines why those explanations may exist.

Together they create independent discussion where ideas are tested from opposite directions.

**Angel's-advocate is a contribution method, not a title or profession.**
