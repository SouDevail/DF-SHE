# ❓☯️ Questioner

### What is a Questioner?

A Questioner is a person and a way to contribute.

Someone who notices what others stop asking. Someone who can make complicated things understandable, even for a person who has never touched the subject before.

And sometimes the most valuable question is the one nobody thought to ask.

You may also ask about the side of— ...DF-SHE that usually stays quiet: an uncomfortable assumption, an unexplored possibility, a strange consequence, a completely abstract thought. If there is something here that makes you think

```"wait, but what if..."``` — don't throw it away just because it sounds unusual.

### What can your question become?

A question does not need to contain a solution.

It can make someone test something.  
It can expose a missing explanation.  
It can lead to an experiment, a documentation change, a new idea or a problem nobody had noticed.

One good question can be enough to start something that did not exist before.

### How to contribute

Start with the exact thing that made you stop.

Write the question as you actually mean it. If it depends on something specific in DF-SHE, point to it. If the question is abstract, give enough context for another person to understand where it came from.

Then ask it publicly through the appropriate DF-SHE channel.

If the discussion produces something worth preserving, don't let it disappear inside the conversation. Turn the useful result into a documented contribution.

### What makes a strong Questioner?

Not knowing everything. ❕

Being willing to ask the question anyway.

A technical question can be valuable. A philosophical one can be valuable. A question from someone completely outside the field can sometimes reveal an assumption that everyone inside the field stopped noticing years ago.

Your questions become part of what you leave behind. Over time, they can show the kinds of problems you notice, the connections you make and the directions you are willing to explore.

**Questioner is a contribution method, not a title or profession.**  
You can use it to develop your own ability while becoming a real part of DF-SHE and its history.
