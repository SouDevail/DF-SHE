# 🎱Idea-creator ♦️

### What is an Idea-creator?

An Idea-creator is a person and a way to contribute.

Someone who sees a possible change where others see a finished structure. Someone who can bring a concept from another field, challenge an assumption or suggest something nobody has tried here before.

You do not need to know every line of code. You need to understand enough to see where your idea could belong.

**Strange is welcome. Meaningless is not.**

### Bring the idea

It can be an architectural change, a new concept, a hypothesis, an experiment worth trying, something to add, something to remove or a connection to a completely different field.

Your idea does not need to be proven before you bring it here. It needs a reason to exist.

A few sentences can be enough:

*Why could this help?*  
*What could it change?*  
*Why is it worth looking at?*

### What happens next?

A validated idea can become a documented DF-SHE hypothesis and remain attached to its creator.

It can later be developed by you, by the community or by SouDevail. It can become an experiment, an architectural change or something completely unexpected.

And if it does not work, that can still teach us where the limits are.

The idea and its history do not disappear.

### Challenge the architecture

You are allowed to question it.

Even an idea that conflicts with the current structure can be worth examining if it still carries the purpose and philosophy of DF-SHE.

A completely unrelated project is simply another project.

A radical change that preserves the core of DF-SHE may be something much more interesting.

**High risk. High reward.**

### You do not need to be a programmer

Biology. Physics. Astronomy. Mathematics. Design. Linguistics. Engineering. Gardening.

The field you come from can change the way you see the architecture.

You may notice a connection that someone inside computer science would never think to make.

That difference is useful.

### How to contribute

Understand the part of DF-SHE your idea touches.

Then write it clearly enough for someone else to understand without reconstructing your thoughts.

Give it a reason.

Submit it publicly through the appropriate DF-SHE channel.

[See how to create an Issue →](../../Assets/classic-github-docs/issue-creating.md)

[See how to use Discussions →](../../Assets/classic-github-docs/github-discussions.md)

If the idea is accepted as a valid contribution, its creator can become part of DF-SHE history as the person who introduced that hypothesis or concept.

### Idea-creator and Researcher

Idea-creator is the theorist.

Researcher is the practitioner.

One creates the hypotheses and theories. The other takes them into practice and finds out what happens.

They can work together. Or they can be the same person.

You may create an idea today and become the one who investigates it tomorrow.

**Idea-creator is a contribution method, not a title or profession.**
