### Efficiency-Tuner — Who is him?

An Efficiency-Tuner is a person and a way to contribute by making existing systems work better without losing what makes them work. We need optimization knowledge.

The role focuses on architecture, code, technical processes and performance — finding precise changes instead of rebuilding without necessity.

### What is improving?

Existing technologies, code paths, architectural details and processes.

The goal is to make them **faster, more efficient and more practical while preserving their logic, technical details and purpose.**

**A useful optimization may:**
⚖️...

I. Remove unnecessary steps or resource usage.

II. Improve speed or practical performance.

III. Work better under limited conditions.

IV. Simplify a technical path without losing important details.

V. Add adjustable parameters instead of fixed behaviour.

VI. Make narrow components replaceable without unnecessary rewrites.

**Find the problem first**

An optimization should have something to improve.

You may discover a tiny problems, unnecessary step or architectural weakness yourself. A theoretical solution is welcome; a working solution, Issue or Pull Request makes the contribution stronger.

[How to create an Issue](../../Assets/classic-github-docs/github-issue-creating.md)

[How to create a Pull Request](../../Assets/classic-github-docs/github-pull-request.md)

**Three things matter 🤌**

**<1> Efficiency** — How much does the change improve speed, resource use and practical performance, including weaker conditions?

**<2> Preservation** — How much of the existing architecture, logic and technical detail remains intact?

**<3> Flexibility** — Does it provide adjustable parameters or narrowly replaceable components without unnecessary rewrites?

### Theory and practice

An Efficiency-Tuner can be both *theorist* and *practitioner*.

You may propose an optimization as a theory, implement it yourself, or do both. An optimization may also build on an Idea-creator's theory, a Researcher's result, existing DF-SHE architecture or another contributor's work. Keep the origin and relevant attribution clear.

If it is rejected

Not every optimization becomes part of the final architecture.

A properly examined proposal can remain in the project's history as a tested possibility for future contributors.

### How small can it be?

A small, noticeable improvement is enough.

Value is not measured by how many files changed. Sometimes the most useful optimization is the smallest change that removes unnecessary cost while preserving everything important.

### Python and AI

Practical Python and optimization experience is strongly useful.

AI may help inspect code, understand unfamiliar systems or develop an optimization. You remain responsible for understanding what changes, why it should work and what its consequences are.

### Efficiency-Tuner and Researcher

[Researcher](https://github.com/SouDevail/DF-SHE/blob/main/DF-TEAM/R-TalentChoose/EXPLORERS/Researcher.md) — practitioner and investigator.
Finds out what happens, under what conditions and why.

**Efficiency-Tuner** — specialized practitioner and theorist.
Finds how an existing technical system can become more efficient while preserving its essential behaviour.

They may work independently or together. One person may be both.

### A different kind of skill

Maybe you are the person who always notices that something could be done faster.

That instinct is not merely criticism. When you turn it into a precise and useful improvement, it becomes a contribution.

**Efficiency-Tuner is a contribution method, not a title or profession.**
