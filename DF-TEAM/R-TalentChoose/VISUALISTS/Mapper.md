# 🌌 Mapper 

**Experience requirement?:** NO.

Creativity and attention to detail matter more.

---

### Mapper — Who is him?

A Mapper is a person and a way to contribute by making the project's structures, connections, ideas and results visible.

This can be a diagram, SVG, animation, interactive visualization, benchmark, statistical view or another visual form.

### What is mapping?

Mapping is not simply making something look good. It turns existing, verifiable information into a useful visual representation without changing its meaning.

**I.** Visualize architecture, systems, processes, concepts or relationships.  
**II.** Create static or animated SVGs and other visual structures.  
**III.** Visualize statistics, benchmarks and research results.  
**IV.** Make complex structures easier to understand.  
**V.** Reveal connections that text alone may hide.  
**VI.** Create visual representations the project does not already have.

---

### Accuracy comes first

A visualization must be supported by the project's documentation, architecture, research or another identified source.

Creativity is welcome. Inventing project details is not.

Even one small, precise visualization can be a complete contribution when it adds something genuinely useful.

### You can map almost anything

Architecture, code structure, processes, concepts, research, benchmarks, statistics, history, experiments and explanations are all valid subjects.

You may also visualize another contributor's idea or research. Keep the original author and source visible.

### Existing visualizations

A Mapper can improve or correct existing visualizations. This may overlap with Documenter, and both roles can work independently or together.

[Docs-Solver](https://github.com/SouDevail/DF-SHE/blob/main/DF-TEAM/R-TalentChoose/Brain-STORMERs/Docs-Solver.md) — explains the information.  
**Mapper — makes the information visible.**

### Technical skills

SVG, XML, animation tools, diagram systems or programming can help, but none are required.

AI-assisted creation is welcome when you understand and verify the final result.

### For beginners too

A Mapper may create visualizations specifically for newcomers, but does not have to.

### When mapping reveals something new

A visualization can expose an unexpected connection, missing component or architectural problem. That discovery may become another contribution or lead to further research.

### The smallest contribution

One carefully made diagram, SVG, corrected connection or visual explanation can be enough.

The size does not decide the value. Accuracy, usefulness and originality do.

### Beyond the repository

Mapping is also encouraged outside GitHub — including X and other public spaces — to show the project's technical, philosophical and creative side.
